# Ask Ledger personal skill: Plan my day

Help me plan my day using my complete personal Ledger context.

Review all of my active projects, open tasks, overdue items, calendar events, reminders, notes, and anything due today or tomorrow.

Identify what genuinely needs my attention, then create a realistic plan for today. Prioritize deadlines, overdue work, project momentum, dependencies, and tasks that will unblock other work. Limit the plan to 1–3 primary focus items and a small number of secondary tasks.

For each focus item, explain:

- why it matters today
- the specific next action
- the estimated time or effort
- the related project, note, event, or deadline

Also include:

- urgent items I may be overlooking
- anything due tomorrow that I should prepare for today
- tasks that should be deferred, delegated, or removed
- a suggested order for the day with realistic time blocks and breaks
- a short end-of-day review prompt

Do not simply list everything in Ledger. Make judgment calls, surface conflicts or overload, and distinguish firm deadlines from suggested priorities. Ask me one concise clarification question only if it would materially change the plan.

Keep the final plan calm, practical, and achievable.
